Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of the mobile game application Neon Strike, which is developed, published, and owned by the developer Tigrisapp (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy clearly identifies the relationship between the application Neon Strike and the developer Tigrisapp as listed on the Google Play Store.

1. Overview and Data Collection

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. The Neon Strike application utilizes specific data processing methods to optimize your gaming experience and save your in-game progress:

  • Guest Mode: When playing the game as a guest, all your game data, scores, and progress are stored strictly locally on your device. No personal data is transmitted outside of your device in this mode.
  • Registration and Login: If you choose to create an account (via email registration) or use the "Sign in with Google" option, authentication processes are handled securely via Firebase Authentication services. In this case, only essential profile information (such as your email address, username, or Google profile ID) is processed to verify your identity and back up your game progress in the cloud.

2. Data Deletion and Account Deletion Right

We believe in giving our users full control over their data. Whether you are a registered user or playing in guest mode, you can permanently and completely delete all your account information, game progress, and associated data at any time directly through the in-game settings menu.

3. Device Permissions Used

To deliver its core gameplay functionalities and ad components seamlessly, Neon Strike requires access to the following device capabilities:

  • INTERNET: Required for the proper functioning of online game components (Firebase integration), secure login operations, and loading advertisements.
  • GET_ACCOUNTS: Used to facilitate secure account verification on the device when the "Sign in with Google" feature is selected.
  • AD_ID (Advertising ID): Required to access the Google Play Services advertising identifier to serve appropriate, personalized, or non-personalized advertisements to the user.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

This application integrates third-party services that may automatically collect information used to identify you or deliver targeted advertisements. Specifically, Neon Strike uses Google AdMob (Google Inc.) to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements within the game.

These third-party providers may automatically collect data such as your IP address, Device Advertising ID (AD_ID), and mobile carrier info to serve relevant ads. For more detailed information on how Google manages your data, please review Google's official privacy framework:

5. Children's Privacy

The Neon Strike application does not knowingly target or address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
