Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Developer: Tigrisapp

Application Title: MahjongPro

At Tigrisapp, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how MahjongPro collects, uses, and safeguards your data when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

To provide a high-quality gaming experience, cloud synchronization, and global leaderboard rankings, MahjongPro processes the following data:

  • Account & Profile Data: Assigned User ID (UID), custom display nickname, and email address (only when authenticated via Google Sign-In or anonymous guest sessions).
  • Gameplay & Progress Statistics: Level completion states, star ratings, earned gold coins, unlocked themes, best completion times, and daily challenge tournament ranks.
  • Technical & Device Info: Operating system version, anonymous device identifiers, and crash diagnostic logs.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

MahjongPro integrates with trusted third-party SDKs to provide backend, monetization, and authentication features:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages secure user authentication, cloud profile storage, and cross-device sync.
  • Google Play Billing / In-App Purchases: Handles secure purchases of In-App Gold Packs and VIP Pass subscriptions. We do not collect or store financial or credit card details on our servers.
  • AdMob & Rewarded Video Ads: Delivers non-personalized advertisements for free users and voluntary rewarded video ads for optional in-game power-up rewards.

3. Children's Privacy Compliance

MahjongPro is a general-audience 3D Mahjong tile matching puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Parents or guardians may contact us at any time to request data removal.

4. Data Rights & Account Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)

Users have full control over their data. You can delete your account and remove all cloud data permanently at any time by selecting Settings -> Delete Account within the app.
